Initial conditions (homogeneous solution)

The homogeneous part is adjusted so the total response satisfies u(0), u̇(0): ηᵣ,h(0) = ηᵣ(0) − ηᵣ,p(0). With zero initial conditions it is the transient that completes the response from rest.
